A disposable two-thread probe paused the first archive at manifest writing and started a second backup: the first failed with `FileNotFoundError` and published nothing; the second succeeded. In addition, the existing destination check followed by `os.replace` (`:267,333`) permits concurrent same-name writers to replace one another despite the intended no-overwrite check. The new interruption test checks a caught `KeyboardInterrupt` in one process; it does not cover active concurrent initialization, same-name writers or forced process death. Preserve live publication ownership and make no-overwrite publication atomic.

### Source recovery leaves an incident open

`operational_assurance.py:359-368` adds `source_evidence` only while failures exist; `_persist_incidents` (`:430-474`) resolves only checks present in the current result. When a failed source recovers, its check disappears and the earlier source incident remains open indefinitely. A disposable probe injected a campaign source failure, recorded health, restored the source, and observed `source_evidence` still `open` after a subsequent incident refresh. The new test verifies that the health check disappears but does not verify the incident resolves. Also, the change immediately reopens any unchanged acknowledged active condition on the next incident GET, making acknowledgement transient. Persist an explicit recovery transition and distinguish acknowledgement from resolution.
